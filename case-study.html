Brand identity and visual design for a modern African fashion designer positioned for a global audience.

ROLEGraphic Designer & Brand Identity Designer
SCOPEIdentity · Campaign · Social · Digital · Print
VISUAL GOALEditorial luxury with African character

Rooted in Africa. Designed for the world.

The system was developed to make the brand feel distinctive without becoming visually complicated. The identity uses a controlled palette, editorial typography, repeatable graphic devices and image direction that can scale from a fashion poster to a digital screen.

One identity. Many touchpoints.

The project demonstrates the exact skill this portfolio is designed to communicate: taking a visual direction and maintaining recognition across campaign compositions, social content, digital presentation and real-world applications.
